Nutritional Principles for Insulin Sensitivity

Low Glycemic Load (GL)

Focus on minimally processed whole carbohydrates that induce a slow, sustained rise in postprandial glucose, reducing compensatory insulin secretion that drives ovarian androgen synthesis.

Adequate Protein & Healthy Fats

Incorporate 20–30g of protein per meal alongside monounsaturated fatty acids (extra virgin olive oil, nuts, seeds) to promote satiety, delay gastric emptying, and suppress excessive ghrelin surges.

Micronutrient Support

Monitor Vitamin D, Magnesium, Inositol, and Omega-3 intake. Inositol (specifically a 40:1 Myo- to D-chiro-inositol ratio) has strong meta-analytic support for restoring ovulatory cycles and improving insulin sensitivity.

Individualized Care

Every individual's metabolic profile, phenotypic presentation (hyperandrogenic vs ovulatory vs metabolic), and body composition are unique. Consult a registered dietitian or certified nutritionist specializing in reproductive endocrinology before starting intensive caloric restrictions or dietary supplements.
