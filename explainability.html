Understanding Explainable AI (XAI) in Ovarian Imaging

1. Gradient Flow

During backward propagation, gradients of the final PCOS class score are computed with respect to the feature activation maps of the final convolutional layer.

2. Global Weighting

Gradients are spatially pooled to determine the exact importance weight of each channel, highlighting feature detectors that correlated with PCOS morphology.

3. Clinical Validation

Physicians can visually audit whether the model concentrated on true ovarian follicles rather than background acoustic shadows, probe calipers, or machine text.

Critical Clinical Caveat: Red and yellow regions indicate high positive mathematical attribution inside the deep neural network. They do not constitute automated segmentation, cystic fluid volume measurement, or diagnostic confirmation.
